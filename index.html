What should each subscriber watch next?

Finding which titles share an audience, and turning that into personal recommendations

Streaming services keep subscribers by helping them find the next show they will love. This project uses viewing time from 2,000 subscribers across 9 titles to find which titles share an audience, then turns those links into a “watch next” recommender anyone can try.

Key findings

  • Dark + Umbrella Academy share the strongest audience link (r = 0.96).
  • You, Dahmer and Dexter form one audience group (average r = 0.90).
  • Dark, Umbrella Academy and Stranger Things form a second group (average r = 0.88).
  • Queen Of Tears and It’s Okay To Not Be Okay share an audience (r = 0.87).
  • Big Bang Theory stands alone: its viewers behave independently of every other title.
